Cezve

A long-handled copper pot for Turkish coffee. The grounds go in with the water and stay there, giving a thick, unfiltered cup.

Usage tips

Grind finer than espresso, almost to a powder. One heaped teaspoon per 60 g of cold water, heated slowly. Lift it off the moment the foam climbs the neck — if it boils, the crema is gone.

$28.00

Espresso Machine

A single group home machine with a 58 mm portafilter and a steam wand. The same pressure profile we pull behind the bar.

Usage tips

Flush the group before every shot and keep the basket dry. Aim for 18 g in and 36 g out in 27 to 32 seconds; if it runs fast, grind finer rather than tamping harder.

$420.00

French Press

Eight cup borosilicate press with a stainless steel mesh. The simplest way to a full, heavy cup.

Usage tips

Coarse grind only, or you will taste silt. Steep four minutes, break the crust, skim the foam, then press slowly and decant straight away.

$38.00

Moka Pot

A six cup aluminium stovetop pot. Strong, concentrated coffee with none of the electricity.

Usage tips

Fill the base to just below the valve with water off the boil, level the basket without tamping, and lift it off the heat the moment it starts to gurgle.

$46.00
